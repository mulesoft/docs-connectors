= A2A Connector 0.2.0-BETA Reference

Anypoint Connector for Agent2Agent (A2A) (A2A Connector) provides A2A protocol support for LLM agents, enabling your agents to talk to other agents.

[NOTE]
====
Anypoint Connector for Agent2Agent (A2A) is a beta connector that is subject to the Beta Services Terms at https://www.salesforce.com/company/legal/[Agreements - Salesforce.com] or a written Unified Pilot Agreement if executed by Customer, and the Non-GA Gen AI and Non-GA Credit Consumption terms in the https://ptd.salesforce.com/?_ga=2.247987783.1372150065.1709219475-629000709.1639001992[Product Terms Directory]. Use of this beta connector with external systems or services consumes xref:general::usage-metrics.adoc[Mule messages] and is at the Customer's sole discretion.

|===
