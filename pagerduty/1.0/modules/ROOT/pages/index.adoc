= {connector-name} Connector 1.0

Anypoint Connector for {connector-name} ({connector-name} Connector) enables you to connect to the {connector-name} API. PagerDuty Connector enables Mule applications to manage the entire incident lifecycle, including triggering, acknowledging, and resolving incidents, and configure core PagerDuty assets like Users, Schedules, and Escalation Policies directly from your Mule flows. 

For information about compatibility and fixed issues, refer to the {connector-name} Connector Release Notes.
